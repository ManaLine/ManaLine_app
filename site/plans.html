Plans

Four tiers, sized by how many people are on the ledger.

Every tier covers the same app — the same collection screens, the same record for every loan. What changes between tiers is how many agents, customers and investors a business can have on it at once.

No price, on purpose
Billing is not live

There is no bill yet, so there is no price to show

MANA LINE does not charge anyone today. There is no payment method on file, nothing is billed monthly or yearly, and nothing here is an offer you can accept by signing up — the tiers below describe capacity, not cost. When billing exists, the prices for each tier will be published here alongside it, not before.

The tiers
Capacity

What each tier covers

Agents are the main thing a tier limits, since each agent's route drives most of the app's daily load. Customer and investor limits are generous on every tier — they exist to bound unusual accounts, not to pinch ordinary use. Enterprise has no stated limit on any of the three; a business that outgrows Business talks to us directly.

Every tier starts the same way

Registering and signing in do not ask which tier you want — a new business starts on the smallest tier that fits the agents, customers and investors it actually has, and moves up only if it grows past that.
